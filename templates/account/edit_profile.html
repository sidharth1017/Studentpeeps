Frequently Asked Questions

What is Studentpeeps?

Studentpeeps is a platform where university students can register and get student discounts on their favourite brands.

How do I get Studentpeeps discount?

To get a Studentpeeps account you MUST be attending a college or University which provides you with an email address or iD card. Go to our website, click on Signup and enter details.

Can I get Studentpeeps without being a student?

No, you can reap StudentPeeps' offer till you’re a student. Enjoy while you’re in college!

Are gift cards refundable?

Gift cards cannot be canceled or returned once issued. Please check terms and conditions before purchase.
