Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about StudentPeeps and your gift cards.

What is StudentPeeps?

StudentPeeps is a platform where university students can register and get student discounts on their favourite brands.

How do I get the StudentPeeps discount?

To get a StudentPeeps account you must be attending a college or university that gives you an email address or ID card. Click Sign in and enter your details.

Can I use StudentPeeps without being a student?

No, StudentPeeps offers are for students. Enjoy them while you’re in college!

Are gift cards refundable?

Gift cards can’t be cancelled or returned once issued. Please check the terms and conditions before you buy.

What if I don’t receive the gift card?

In the rare case, it happens. Don’t worry — just email us at hi@studentpeeps.club. We will either send the code or refund the amount in full.
